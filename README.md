# Residence times in haematopoiesis: Busch et al. (2015), re-implementation and model extension

![Licence: MIT](https://img.shields.io/badge/licence-MIT-lightgrey)

## TL;DR

**This repository revisits a published model of haematopoiesis from [Busch et al. (2015)](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature14242)**: (1) re-implementation of the original model, (2) exploration of its assumptions and limitations, and (3) model extension by proposing a new hypothesis about the regulatory mechanisms underlying blood cell development.

This work was originally carried out in October 2019 as part of the *Computational Systems Biology of Cancer* project course at the Biology Institute of [ENS-PSL](https://www.ibens.bio.ens.psl.eu/) and [Institut Curie](https://curie.fr/), in context of the Master in Life Sciences program.

## Data

**Note:** The 2019 version of this project ran on the authors' measurements. Label-progression data of Fig. 3c of Busch et al. (2015) were provided for the course by the paper's co-author Prof. Thomas Höfer (DKFZ) as they are unpublished.

**This repository does not contain the raw unpublished data.** Hence, by default everything runs on [synthetic data](data/synthetic).
